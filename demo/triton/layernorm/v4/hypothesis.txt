Hypothesis: Make D a tl.constexpr to enable compile-time optimizations (reciprocal division, reduction unrolling)
Rationale : v0 NCU shows the kernel is memory-bound (Memory SOL=84.72%), but WarpExecEff=32% and IPC=0.12
            suggest inefficiency in the reduction computation. D is used for integer division (mean=sum/D,
            var=sum_sq/D) — with runtime D, Triton generates expensive integer division. As constexpr,
            the compiler can use multiply-by-reciprocal and better unroll the 1024-element reduction.
            Same memory traffic as v0 but more efficient computation.
Expected  : IPC ↑ modestly, latency ↓ 3-8%.
