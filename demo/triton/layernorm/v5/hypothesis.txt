Hypothesis: Remove mask and other= parameters from loads/stores when D is power-of-2 (BLOCK_SIZE==D, mask always True)
Rationale : v0 uses masked loads with other=0.0 even though D=1024 is power-of-2 and BLOCK_SIZE=1024=D,
            meaning the mask is always True. The mask check and other-value clamping add overhead:
            predicate evaluation per load/store, register holding neutral value. Removing them
            allows the compiler to generate unmasked ld.global/st.global without predication,
            potentially reducing instruction count and register pressure.
Expected  : Registers/thread ↓ slightly, IPC ↑, latency ↓ 2-5%.
