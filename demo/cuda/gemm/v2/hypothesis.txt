Hypothesis: Fuse 2 K-tile loads per barrier cycle to reduce __syncthreads() overhead
Rationale : v1 NCU shows Barrier stall=5.96 is the dominant bottleneck from 128 total
            __syncthreads() calls (2 per K-tile iteration × 64 tiles).
            Loading and computing 2 tiles per barrier reduces sync calls from 128→64.
            Experience log confirms this gave 1.02x speedup with Long Scoreboard
            dropping 44% (more compute per barrier hides load latency).
Expected  : Barrier stall ↓ moderately, Long Scoreboard ↓ significantly (more ILP
            between barriers), latency ↓ 2-5%. Shared memory usage doubles (2KB→4KB).
