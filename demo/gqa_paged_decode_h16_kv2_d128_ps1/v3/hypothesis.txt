- Scope: Grid definition, 4 sets of (m, l, acc) instead of 2
- Things intentionally not changed: num_warps=8, BLOCK_D=128, online softmax, page-outer loop, BF16

Rationale:
- Why this should address the measured bottleneck: Each KV page load now serves 4 dot products instead of 2, halving the per-head memory traffic again

Expected metric movement:
- Improve: Execution time, memory throughput, L1 hit rate
- Watch for regressions: Grid at 64 blocks may show slight occupancy drop; 4 accumulators increase register usage

- Correctness risk: Low — same algorithm structure, just 4 independent Q-head computations
